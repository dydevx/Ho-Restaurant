# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Guests in and around Mindelheim who want to discover Ho Restaurant, browse its Vietnamese and sushi menu, and prepare a takeaway order through WhatsApp.

## Product Purpose

Present the restaurant, its food, opening information, and contact details clearly while making the current menu easy to browse and order from. Success means guests can quickly understand the offer, choose dishes, and contact the restaurant with confidence.

## Brand Personality

Welcoming, refined, and authentic. The experience should feel warm and polished while staying practical for guests who primarily need menu and ordering information.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic delivery-marketplace styling, novelty Asian visual stereotypes, and redesigns that obscure prices or ordering details. Preserve the restaurant's established dark, gold-accented visual identity.

## Design Principles

- Keep food, prices, and ordering actions immediately understandable.
- Preserve the existing restaurant identity and visual hierarchy.
- Let real dishes and restaurant photography carry the atmosphere.
- Make mobile browsing and takeaway ordering effortless.
- Treat German and Vietnamese dish names accurately and respectfully.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain WCAG AA contrast where practical, semantic controls and labels, keyboard usability, readable mobile layouts, and reduced-motion support. Do not rely on color alone to communicate dietary or spice information.
